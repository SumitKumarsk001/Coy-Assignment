# Part A: Two feature suggestions

> Replace the "What I noticed" lines with your own observations from using the app.

## 1. Anonymous interest signal
**What I noticed:** _(e.g. after adding a crush, nothing happens until they join or add me back)_
**Feature:** Show a user an anonymous count ("someone has added you") with no identity, only after a minimum threshold, plus an anonymous nudge they can send to a crush.
**Why:** Mutual-only reveal means long silent periods, which hurts retention. A count gives a reason to return and to invite friends. A threshold stops people deducing who it is.
**Privacy:** Counts are computed server-side and never linked to an ID in any response.

## 2. Crush expiry and "still interested?" check-in
**What I noticed:** _(e.g. crush slots are limited and I could not tell which ones were stale)_
**Feature:** Crushes expire after ~30 days, with a reminder before expiry to renew or let go. Letting go frees the slot.
**Why:** Stale crushes waste limited slots, so buying extra slots feels like paying to fix clutter. Expiry brings users back regularly and keeps the list honest.
**Privacy:** Expired crushes are deleted, so old intent doesn't sit in the system forever.
